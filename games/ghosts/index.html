How to play

Each player has 8 ghosts: 4 good and 4 evil. You secretly choose which is which before the game, and you can see only your own roles — an enemy ghost looks the same whether it is good or evil until it is captured.

On your turn, move one ghost one square up, down, left or right. Moving onto an enemy ghost captures it and reveals what it was. You win if you capture all 4 of the enemy's good ghosts, or if you walk one of your good ghosts off the board through one of the two gates in the enemy's back corners (step onto the glowing corner square, then out through the gate). You lose if you end up capturing all 4 of the enemy's evil ghosts.
