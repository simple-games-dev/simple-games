How to play

On your turn do one of four things: take 3 gems of different colours; take 2 gems of the same colour (only if at least 4 of that colour are in the bank); reserve a card (face-up, or blind from a deck) and take a gold joker; or buy a card.

Cards you own give a permanent bonus of their colour — a discount on everything you buy later. Gold gems are wildcards. You may hold at most 10 gems and 3 reserved cards.

At the end of your turn, if your bonuses meet a noble's requirements, that noble visits you for 3 prestige (if several qualify you choose one). When someone reaches 15 prestige the round is finished so everyone has the same number of turns; the highest score wins, ties go to whoever bought fewer cards.

Card values and nobles follow the standard base-game list.
