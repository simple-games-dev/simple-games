How to play

Be the first to walk your pawn to the opposite side of the 9×9 board. On your turn you do one of two things: step your pawn one square up, down, left or right, or place one of your hedges (each is two squares long) in a gap between squares to block movement.

If an enemy pawn is right in front of you, you can jump over it. If a hedge or the edge is behind it, you can hop diagonally to either side instead. Hedges can't overlap or cross, and you may never wall off a player completely — everyone must always keep at least one path to their goal.

To place a hedge, drag one of the two hedge pieces (across or up/down) from the tray onto the board and let go in the gap you want. You can also tap a piece to pick its direction, aim at a gap and click; R or a right-click turns it. Green means the spot is legal, red means it isn't.
