How to play

On your turn, move one pawn to an empty neighbouring tile (including diagonals). You can step down any number of storeys, stay level, or climb at most one storey up — never onto a capped, four-storey roof. Then build one storey on an empty tile next to your pawn's new position, if a legal one exists. Reach the third storey and you win instantly. If none of your pawns can move on your turn, you're out of the game — last builder standing wins.
